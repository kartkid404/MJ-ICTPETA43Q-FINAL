ABOUT US:

Welcome to Aerial Journey, your gateway to the skies and beyond! Founded with a dream to make air travel more accessible and enjoyable, Aerial Journey has grown to become a trusted name in the airline industry, connecting the Philippines to the world.

Aerial Journey was established in 1995 by two lifelong friends, Jerian Morales and Michael Trampe, both passionate advocates for travel and aviation. Michael, a skilled businessman from Manila, and Jerian, a travel enthusiast from Cebu, combined their talents to create an airline agency that would not only serve travelers but also highlight the beauty of the Philippines on the global stage.

The idea for Aerial Journey was born during a chance reunion in 1994. Over a cup of coffee, Michael shared his vision of simplifying air travel, while Jerian spoke of her dream to connect people to the Philippines' rich culture and breathtaking destinations. Together, they decided to turn their dreams into reality by founding Aerial Journey.

Why We Started:

Michael and Jerian were driven by a shared experience: both had seen firsthand the challenges of affordable and accessible air travel, particularly for those wanting to explore the Philippines. They recognized the potential of the country’s tourism industry and believed that a customer-focused agency could make a difference.

Their vision was simple yet ambitious:

  • Affordable Travel: Offer competitive pricing for local and international flights.
  • Customer Experience: Simplify booking processes and provide exceptional support.
  • Cultural Promotion: Share the Philippines' stunning landscapes and vibrant culture with the world.

Where You Can Find Us:

What started as a small office in Manila quickly grew into a nationwide and international network. Today, Aerial Journey operates in major Philippine cities, including Cebu, Davao, and Boracay, and has expanded globally with offices in:

  • Singapore
  • Dubai, UAE
  • Tokyo, Japan
  • Sydney, Australia
  • Los Angeles, USA
  • Manila, Philippines

Our Services:

At Aerial Journey, we offer a wide range of travel services tailored to your needs:

  • Domestic and international flight bookings.
  • Exclusive vacation packages to top destinations in the Philippines.
  • Corporate travel management for businesses.
  • 24/7 customer support to ensure peace of mind for travelers.

Our Vision and Mission


Vision: To be the leading airline agency in Asia, recognized for excellence, innovation, and a commitment to showcasing the beauty of the Philippines.
Mission: To make air travel seamless, affordable, and unforgettable, while proudly promoting the Philippines as a must-visit destination.

Aerial Journey Today

Nearly three decades after its founding, Aerial Journey continues to thrive under the leadership of Michael and Jerian, who remain deeply involved in the company. The agency boasts a team of over 500 dedicated travel experts and partnerships with top airlines around the globe.
Whether you're flying to explore the majestic Rice Terraces of Banaue, the crystal-clear waters of Palawan, or embarking on an international adventure, Aerial Journey is here to make your dream journey a reality.
